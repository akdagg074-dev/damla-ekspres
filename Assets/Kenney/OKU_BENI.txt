Bu klasordeki tum gorseller ve 3D modeller Kenney (www.kenney.nl) tarafindan
Creative Commons CC0 (kamu malı) lisansiyla yayinlanmistir. Ticari ve egitim
amacli serbestce kullanilabilir; kaynak gostermek zorunlu degildir.

Kullanilan paketler:
- Car Kit (3D)                : Modeller/CarKit      -> su tankeri (pikap)
- City Kit Commercial (3D)    : Modeller/CityCommercial -> Damla Su deposu, apartmanlar
- City Kit Suburban (3D)      : Modeller/CitySuburban  -> musteri evleri
- Background Elements Remastered (2D): Sprites/Cevre, Sprites/ArkaPlan
- New Platformer Pack (2D)    : Sprites/Zemin, Sprites/Dekor, Sprites/ArkaPlan
- UI Pack (2D)                : Assets/Resources/UI (buton, panel, kaydirici, yildiz, font, tik sesi)

3D modeller, Editor/ModelRender.cs ile yandan render edilip dis hat (outline)
eklenerek 2D sprite'a cevrilir (Assets/Sprites/Render).
